REFERENCE LEVELS

Strength benchmark multipliers

Relative load = recorded external load ÷ latest bodyweight. Target load = latest bodyweight × the level multiplier. Repetitions are shown but are not converted into estimated maxes. Edit these rough references to suit your training; they are not validated dunking thresholds.

STRENGTH DEVELOPMENT

Lift history and estimated reference targets

Record the external load used for the listed reps. Bulgarian split squats are recorded per hand; other lifts are recorded as total external load. Trap bar jumps are kept separate from regular strength lifts: use controlled, fast reps and appropriate technique rather than chasing heavier loads.
